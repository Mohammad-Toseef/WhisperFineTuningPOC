## ✅ ROUND-1 ADAPTER BACKED UP — and it was a genuine single point of failure
User asked what happens to round 1's model if round 2 goes wrong. Two-part answer.

### Training cannot damage round 1 — by design AND by enforcement
`run_tag: r2` sends every write to a new path (adapter, merged model, checkpoints, tb logs,
eval_results, dataset), and round 1's adapter is only ever **read** (`PeftModel.from_pretrained`),
which cannot modify it. If `run_tag` were ever removed, the #3 collision guard **raises before
training starts** — and since it raises at setup, the merged-model path is never reached either. One
guard blocks the whole class.

Crucially: **round 2 cannot produce a "damaged round 1".** Forgetting is a property of the NEW
adapter; round 1's files are byte-identical whatever happens. Crash / OOM / timeout / Ctrl-C /
full volume all leave round 1 untouched — they only cost round 2 its own output.

The one real exposure is **operator error on the upload command**, not the code: a typo dropping
`_r2` from the *remote* path in `modal volume put … --force` would overwrite round 1's dataset. Low
severity — it also exists locally (1.25 GB) and is regenerable.

### ⚠️ The gap: the adapter existed in ONE place
`scripts/push_to_hub.py:19` pushes only `/model/whisper-urdu-final`. So the merged model had two
copies (volume + HF Hub) but the **adapter had one** — and it is exactly what round 2 resumes from,
on a volume `CLAUDE.md` explicitly says is not permanent. `Downloads\Whisper Smoke Test Trained
Model\` turned out to be the **smoke test's** artifacts, so the full run's adapter had never been
backed up. Losing it would keep round 1 usable for inference but end the ability to *continue* from
it: LoRA A/B factors cannot be cleanly recovered from a merged model.

Backed up to `Downloads\Whisper Round1 Full Run Adapter\whisper-urdu-lora-adapter`, 10 files,

separately from the volume (sha256 `8689f3c8…`), `r=32 / alpha=64 / 6 Tier-2 targets`.
